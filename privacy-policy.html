Welcome to Kissht Bahi ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal and business data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information about you in a variety of ways when you use the Kissht Bahi app:

  • Personal Data: When you register an account, we collect your name, email address, business/shop name, and authentication details.
  • Business & Customer Data: Information you input regarding your customers, loans, installments, late fees, and staff PINs to manage your financial records.
  • Device & Security Data: Biometric permissions (fingerprint/Face ID) or device security states strictly used for enabling the local App Lock feature on your device.

2. Use of Your Information

Having accurate information permits us to provide you with a smooth, efficient, and customized experience. Specifically, we may use information collected about you to:

  • Create and manage your account and authenticate your login.
  • Store and process your shop’s customer, loan, and installment data securely using cloud databases (Supabase).
  • Enable app security features such as Biometric App Lock and Staff PINs.
  • Generate business reports, receipts, and Excel/CSV data exports as requested by you.
  • Communicate with you regarding updates, premium subscriptions, customer support, or technical notices.

3. Disclosure of Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal or business data to third parties. We may share information only in the following situations:

  • By Law or to Protect Rights: If required to respond to legal processes, court orders, or government regulations.
  • Service Providers: With trusted cloud infrastructure and database providers (such as Supabase) necessary to host and secure your app data.

4. Security of Your Information

We use administrative, technical, and physical security measures to help protect your personal and business data:

  • All data transmitted to and from our cloud servers is encrypted using standard protocols (HTTPS/SSL).
  • App Lock features ensure that unauthorized individuals cannot access your financial logs without biometric or device-level authentication.
  • While we have taken reasonable steps to secure your data, please note that no electronic transmission over the internet or storage technology is 100% secure.

5. Policy for Children

We do not knowingly solicit information from or market to children under the age of 18. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a minor without verification of parental consent, we will delete that information as quickly as possible.
